TOP PHILIPPINE ISLANDS

☀ ★ ☀

🌊 SIARGAO ISLAND

Mindanao • Surfing Capital

Known as the Surfing Capital of the Philippines, Siargao invites thrill-seekers to ride the world-famous barrel waves of Cloud 9. Beyond surfing, you can explore enchanting emerald lagoons, swing from coconut palms into crystal-clear rivers, and hop between untouched white-sand islets. Its laid-back island vibe and warm local hospitality make it the ultimate tropical escape for adventure lovers!

🏝️ PALAWAN

Luzon • Last Frontier

Repeatedly voted one of the most beautiful islands in the world, Palawan boasts towering limestone cliffs and hidden turquoise lagoons. You can kayak through the secret coves of El Nido, snorkel over historic shipwrecks in Coron, or cruise down the awe-inspiring Puerto Princesa Underground River. Every corner of this paradise feels like stepping straight into a vibrant postcard!

⛰️ BOHOL

Visayas • Natural Wonders

Bohol is home to the iconic Chocolate Hills, where over a thousand cone-shaped mounds turn a rich cocoa brown during the dry season. Visitors can also meet the tiny, wide-eyed Philippine tarsiers in lush sanctuaries and enjoy a scenic floating restaurant cruise along the Loboc River. With Panglao’s white beaches just minutes away, Bohol blends natural wonders with relaxing coastal charm!

🏖️ BORACAY

Visayas • White Beachs

Famous for its four-kilometer stretch of powdery White Beach, Boracay offers sand so soft and cool it feels like walking on clouds. By day, you can go parasailing over turquoise waters or try paddleboarding along the calm shoreline. By night, the island comes alive with golden sunsets, beachfront dining, and vibrant island music!

✨ SIQUIJOR

Visayas • Island of Fire

Famed for its mystical charm and untouched natural beauty, Siquijor is a tranquil island of cascading waterfalls and centuries-old balete trees. You can swim in the tiered turquoise pools of Cambugahay Falls, explore marine sanctuaries teeming with sea turtles, and unwind on quiet white-sand beaches. Its peaceful atmosphere and enchanting folklore make it a truly magical getaway!
